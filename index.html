The Idea

Ownership, not access

Most games today are rented to you — tied to a launcher, an account, or a live-service switch someone else controls. We think that's backwards.

"When you have one of our games, it's yours. Not licensed to you. Not tied to a service that can disappear. Yours — to keep, to copy, to hand to a friend on a USB drive if you want to."
— The Pie Time Studio Manifesto
How we survive

We run entirely on donations

No investors, no ad networks, no store cuts baked into a price tag. Just people who play our games and decide it's worth chipping in. If that sounds impossible — that's kind of the point.

🫶

Patronage-first

Development is funded by ongoing donations and community support, not by selling access to the games themselves.

🔍

Transparent by default

We want supporters to see where their support goes — that's part of the deal we're building.

🌱

Small, sustainable, honest

We're not chasing hyper-growth. We're testing whether honest games can support themselves.
